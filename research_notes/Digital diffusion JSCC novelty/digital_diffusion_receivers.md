# Digital (finite-constellation / quantised) JSCC for images with a diffusion or flow receiver: verified literature map (2022 to Nov 2026)

Search date: 2026-11-08. Verification levels used below:
- **[FT]**: full text (arXiv HTML or PDF) fetched; quotes come from it. Quotes passed through a summarising fetch tool, so treat them as near-verbatim and re-check the exact wording before citing in a paper.
- **[ABS]**: abstract or arXiv landing page only. Abstract only, unverified.
- **[SNIP]**: search-engine snippet or a citing paper's description only. Unverified.

## Q1. Does any paper quantise DiT-JSCC's (or a similar "generate from pure noise, conditioned on received symbols" diffusion-JSCC's) channel symbols to a QAM/PSK constellation?

### Takeaway
No such paper turned up. DiT-JSCC itself is analog: complex symbols with power normalisation, one model per SNR, and QAM appears only in its LDPC baselines. The pure-noise conditional-generator diffusion JSCCs we checked (DiT-JSCC, DiffJSCC, CDM-JSCC, LRISC, DiffCom) all send continuous symbols. The newest QAM-native generative JSCC (SemQAM, Oct 2026) uses no diffusion receiver. It uses a diffusion codec (DiT-IC) only as a separated baseline with LDPC and QAM.

### Cited Findings
- **DiT-JSCC (arXiv:2601.03112, Jan 2026, revised Jun 2026; listed as IEEE TCCN on ResearchGate) [FT]**. Sec. II-A: "encodes the image x into a vector of complex-valued channel input symbols s∈ℂ^k"; "After power normalization which ensures an average power constraint, these k symbols are transmitted". Digital modulation appears only for baselines (Sec. IV-A3): separation schemes "combine them with 5G LDPC channel coding" with "4-ary quadrature amplitude modulation (4QAM)". The fetch found no use of "quantization", "constellation" or "DeepJSCC-Q", and the future work (Sec. V) does not mention digital transmission. SNR (Sec. IV-A4): "we train separate models for each SNR under both AWGN and Rayleigh fading channels". Data: ImageNet at 256×256 or 512×512, tested on 100 ImageNet-val images. Code: github.com/semcomm/DiTJSCC — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.03112), [ResearchGate venue listing](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/407502169_DiT-JSCC_Rethinking_Deep_JSCC_With_Diffusion_Transformers_and_Semantic_Representations). Caveat: the fetch tool read only the first 100k of 120k characters, so the last part (probably references and appendix) was not searched.
- **SemQAM: "Joint Semantic-Channel Coding and Modulation for Generative Image Transmission" (Cao, Ying, Huang, Qin, Shen; Tsinghua; arXiv:2610.29643v1, 21 Oct 2026) [FT]**. This is the closest "digital generative JSCC", but it has no diffusion receiver. Sec. 3.1: "Each codebook contains 1024 codewords whose indices are mapped one to one to Gray-labeled 1024-QAM symbols"; also "without separate channel coding between VQ and modulation". Residual VQ on the main latent and the hyperlatent. The "generative" part comes from training losses (Sec. 3.3: "the generator-side PatchGAN loss following VQGAN"), plus LPIPS. SNR handling (Sec. 4.1): "SemQAM uses four CBR settings for each of 10 dB, 16 dB, 24 dB, and noiseless transmission, giving sixteen codebook groups". The networks are shared and codebooks are switched per operating point, so the model is not blind. Baselines DiT-IC and OSCAR are "generative image codecs paired with LDPC and QAM with MCS table". Related work: "DeepJSCC-Q constrains channel symbols to finite constellations". The fetched part does not mention DiT-JSCC or DiffCom. Code: "will be publicly released upon acceptance". Data: trained on ImageNet; tested on Kodak, Tecnick, DIV2K-val and CLIC2020 — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29643)
- **DeepJSCC-Q (Tung, Shiu, Gündüz; arXiv:2206.08100; earlier 2111.13042)** is the finite-constellation JSCC we would build on. It has no diffusion — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.08100) [SNIP for content; well known]
- **Distribution-Aware Constellation Learning (DLM-SC; Zhang, Huang, Ying, Liu, Qin; arXiv:2605.30988, 29 May 2026) [ABS]**. A learnable constellation "initialized with an amplitude phase shift keying geometric prior" and trained with "differentiable soft assignment with straight-through estimator". The abstract does not mention a diffusion or generative receiver — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30988)

### Inferences
- We found no work that quantises the symbols of a pure-noise conditional diffusion JSCC receiver (the DiT-JSCC / DiffJSCC / CDM-JSCC family) to M-QAM or PSK, for example with a DeepJSCC-Q soft-to-hard quantiser. Within this search, "DiT-JSCC + M-QAM via DeepJSCC-Q" looks open. The nearest neighbours that need careful positioning are SemQAM (QAM-native but GAN/VQ, Oct 2026) and the denoise-the-constellation papers (SCDM, SSCDM, CD3M) covered in Q3.
- SemQAM and DLM-SC both come from Zhijin Qin's group (Tsinghua) within six months. This group is the most likely to publish "SemQAM + diffusion decoder" next, so it is the main scooping risk.

### Gaps
- Google Scholar and Semantic Scholar "cited by" lists for DiT-JSCC, DiffCom and DeepJSCC-Q could not be checked: Semantic Scholar's API returned HTTP 429, and Scholar was not attempted. A citer of DiT-JSCC published after about Oct 2026 could be missed.
- We could not check whether the DiT-JSCC TCCN journal version (if it differs from arXiv v2) adds a digital variant.

## Q2. Which digital diffusion semantic-communication papers have the diffusion model generate the image conditioned on received digital symbols or bits, rather than refining a decoded image?

### Takeaway
Papers where a diffusion model generates the image from digital information do exist, but all of them we verified use separation: discrete tokens, prompts or captions sent over a classical or unspecified channel code and modulation, with the diffusion model conditioned on error-free or CRC-checked decoded content. None conditions a diffusion generator directly on noisy learned-JSCC QAM symbols. DSRDM is a different idea: it hides bits inside a diffusion forward process.

### Cited Findings
- **PDSC: "Personalized Digital Semantic Communication for Image Transmission with Vision-Language Models" (Li, Zhou, Wang, Xiong, Zhao, Wei; arXiv:2608.14260, 14 Aug 2026; "Accepted by IEEE GLOBECOM 2026") [FT]**. Uses VQ tokens (Sec. II-A, "Each semantic token z_j is mapped to its nearest codeword") and "fixed-length binary coding" (L=21, K=1024, so 210 bits/image). Sec. III-D: the bits are sent "with conventional channel coding and modulation", but the paper names no code or QAM order (LDPC appears only in the BPG+LDPC baseline). The latent diffusion denoiser is conditioned on the recovered tokens (Eq. 28). The fetch did not establish whether sampling starts from pure noise. AWGN, 0 to 20 dB. Dataset: MovieLens posters. No code link — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14260), [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14260)
- **Text-Guided Token Communication (Liu, Qiao, Wang, Gao, et al., BIT; arXiv:2507.05781, 8 Jul 2025) [FT]**. TA-TiTok VQ tokens, then "polar coding following the 5G NR standard" with SCL-8 decoding, then "We employ 4-QAM constellation modulation". Tokens that fail CRC are masked and refilled by MaskGen, a masked generative transformer that the paper does not call diffusion, using an "iterative confidence-based approach". ImageNet at 256×256, AWGN −5 to 5 dB, no code link. This is a classical-code digital system with a generative token in-painter, not a diffusion model — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.05781)
- **Latency-Aware Generative SemCom with pre-trained diffusion models (Qiao et al.; arXiv:2403.17256, 2024) [SNIP]**. A prompt is sent with "re-transmission-based scheme" and other modalities with "an adaptive modulation/coding scheme". Pre-trained diffusion generators run at the receiver. Classical digital separation — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.17256)
- **Generative video SemCom via multimodal semantics (arXiv:2502.13838, 2025) [SNIP]**. Per the snippet, it uses turbo coding with QAM, and a diffusion model fuses text and structural semantics. Classical digital separation. Video, so outside the image scope — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.13838)
- **Diffusion-Aided Bandwidth-Efficient SemCom with Adaptive Requests (Wang, Xie, Li, Liu; arXiv:2510.26442, Oct 2025, v4 Jan 2026) [ABS]**. "transmits a short caption together with an initial sparse subset of latent blocks"; "the receiver reconstructs the image via latent diffusion inpainting". The abstract states no modulation. A search snippet claimed 16-QAM, but we did not verify this — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26442)
- **DSRDM: Digital Signal Recovery Diffusion Model (Yan, Li; MUST; arXiv:2605.27730, 26 May 2026, a letter) [FT]**. Bits are mapped to 16-QAM or 256-QAM, then "the original digital signal is converted into an approximate Gaussian signal". "DSRDM adds Gaussian signals to images across multiple steps of the forward process, instead of adding Gaussian noise", and at the receiver "the trained DM is used to predict the added Gaussian noise at each step". The image is a carrier. The payload is arbitrary bits (BER is the metric), so this is not image JSCC. Uses LSUN-Bedroom 256, ImageNet64 and CIFAR-10; AWGN and Rician with perfect CSI. It cites an MAE-based precursor [14] and "Model Shift Modulation" [15] — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.27730)
- **Adapting Diffusion Language Models for Lossless Pixel-Level Image Transmission (DDM-SSCC; Ren, Li, Chen, Li, Zhao; arXiv:2606.06273, 4 Jun 2026) [FT]**. Explicitly separated ("a separate source-channel coding (SSCC) architecture"), with a "rate-1/2 channel code together with an ECCT-enhanced receiver". The discrete diffusion LM is the source probability model for arithmetic coding, not a generator. It cites DiT-JSCC: "DiT-JSCC employs generative diffusion modeling to improve perceptual reconstruction quality under bandwidth- or SNR-limited channels" — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.06273)
- **MOC-RVQ: Multilevel Codebook-assisted Digital Generative SemCom (Zhou, Sun, Chen, Xu, Chen, Huang, Cui, Zhang; arXiv:2401.01272, GLOBECOM 2024) [ABS]**. "Traditional codebooks need wide index ranges, while modulation favors few discrete states"; the system is "directly compatible with digital transmission systems". The generative restoration is a Swin-Transformer noise-reduction block plus the codebook, not diffusion. Code: github.com/Albert2X/moc_rvq — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01272)

### Inferences
- For "a diffusion generator conditioned on received digital symbols", every verified example uses a classical code with clean or CRC-masked tokens (PDSC, Text-Guided TokCom, Qiao, the video paper). None trains a learned soft-to-hard QAM JSCC end to end with a pure-noise conditional DiT receiver. This is the difference our DiT-JSCC + DeepJSCC-Q prototype would claim.
- "Digital generative" papers that are not diffusion (SemQAM, MOC-RVQ, VQ-DeepSC) should be cited as the digital-generative baseline family.

### Gaps
- For PDSC we could not confirm the channel code or modulation, whether channel errors are simulated at bit level, or whether LDM sampling starts from pure noise.
- For 2510.26442, 2403.17256 and 2502.13838 we have only snippets or abstracts for the modulation details.

## Q3. Which "diffusion-based digital semantic communication" papers use diffusion to denoise or demap the received constellation symbols themselves?

### Takeaway
This branch is well populated and is the main prior art to cite: SCDM (continuous score SDE on received QAM symbols, ICC 2025), CD3M (discrete diffusion over constellation indices, IEEE WCL 2026), DCDDM (He et al., RVQ diffusion JSCC, IEEE TWC 2025), and SSCDM (Markov-enforced discrete diffusion, Huh/Kang/Choi, 2026, accepted). All of them pair a learned VQ / joint coding-modulation codec with a diffusion symbol cleaner placed before a deterministic decoder. In all of them, SNR sets the diffusion starting step. None generates the image from pure noise.

### Cited Findings
- **SCDM: Score-Based Channel Denoising Model for Digital SemCom (Mo, Sun, Yao, Chen, Chen, Xu, Tao, Cui; arXiv:2501.17876, 18 Jan 2025; IEEE ICC 2025, pp. 3772–3778 per SSCDM's reference list) [FT]**. The encoder outputs constellation symbols drawn from M points. The experiments use 64-QAM with n = 128, and the codec is the learned JCM (joint coding-modulation) VAE. Sec. II: "The receiver next adopt our propesed SCDM to denoise" the received symbols before the semantic decoder. Sec. III-B drops the drift term because it "would lead to the variance of the constellation points collapsing around the origin", and "The denoising steps N_SNR depend on the channel conditions". One SCDM covers −18 to 18 dB, with the codec pretrained at 18 dB. CIFAR-10 at 32×32. No code stated — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.17876v1)
- **SSCDM: "Markov-Enforced Discrete Diffusion Model for Digital Semantic Symbol Error Correction" (Huh, Kang, Choi; SNU; arXiv:2603.22983v2, 11 Sep 2026; footnote says "accepted 9 September 2026", IEEE journal, editor L. Zhou) [FT, PDF extracted locally]**. A VQ codebook whose size equals the modulation order. Experiments: "We assume 16-QAM modulation for transmission, leading to a codebook size of 16", on FFHQ at 256×256. The JSCC is trained at a fixed 20 dB, and the DM covers −3 to 15 dB with T = 100 steps. "the receiver employs the SSCDM, i.e., DM, to correct symbol errors prior to image reconstruction". The DM input is codeword embeddings, not raw indices, and a SOM loss gives Gray-like codebook topology. Its related work is the best survey of this branch, quoted below — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.22983)
  - Two directions: "symbol error correction using discrete DMs and symbol denoising using continuous DMs".
  - On CD3M [15]: "the channel denoising discrete diffusion model (CD3M) proposed in [15] ... estimates per-step transition matrices by identifying the best-fitting multiplier between two consecutive symbol detection matrices". Ref [15]: W. Tang, Z. Meng, Q. Li, M. Yan, X. Ge, "Channel denoising in digital semantic communication systems via discrete diffusion," IEEE Wireless Communications Letters, vol. 15, pp. 1035–1039, 2026.
  - On DCDDM [16]: "discrete channel denoising diffusion model (DCDDM) proposed in [16]. It adopts a discrete DM framework and constructs per-step transition matrices heuristically". Ref [16]: G. He, S. Zhang, T. Jiang, "Residual vector quantized diffusion model-based joint source-channel coding for task-oriented agent communication," IEEE TWC, vol. 24, no. 6, pp. 4663–4676, 2025.
  - On SCDM [17]: "It directly denoises noisy digital symbols, such as QAM symbols corrupted by Gaussian noise, prior to receiver-side symbol detection ... the diffusion module primarily serves as a noise suppressor rather than a semantics-aware component."
  - It also cites MOC-RVQ [18] for a heuristic codeword-reordering (Gray coding) step.
- **CD3M (Tang, Meng, Li, Yan, Ge; IEEE WCL vol. 15, 2026, pp. 1035–1039; IEEE Xplore doc 11305152)**. Only the SSCDM description and a search snippet are available: per the snippet, it "uses a discrete Markov chain over digital constellations, combined with a VQVAE into a single codec pair". The IEEE page returned empty, so this is unverified — [IEEE Xplore PDF link](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/iel8/5962382/11304577/11305152.pdf), [SSCDM description](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.22983)
- **DCDDM / RVQ diffusion JSCC (He, Zhang, Jiang; IEEE TWC 2025)**. Known only through SSCDM's description. Unverified, and its data are task-oriented "agent communication", which may not be image reconstruction.
- **Extended Universal JSCC for Digital SemCom (Kim, Huh, Choi; arXiv:2602.14018, 2026) [SNIP]**. Digital JSCC over BPSK, 4-QAM and 16-QAM with channel-adaptive codebooks. No diffusion according to the snippet — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14018)
- **Probabilistic constellation shaping with DDPMs (arXiv:2309.08688) [SNIP]**. Diffusion generates constellation symbols for shaping. This is not image JSCC — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.08688)

### Inferences
- The "diffusion as a constellation-symbol denoiser" role is taken (SCDM 2025, CD3M 2026, DCDDM 2025, SSCDM 2026). A DiT-JSCC-Q contribution must not be framed as "first diffusion receiver for digital SemCom". The defensible framing is: "first pure-noise conditional generative diffusion receiver (DiT) for a learned finite-constellation JSCC", with the received QAM symbols, or soft demapper outputs, used only as a condition.
- All of the symbol-denoiser papers use small or medium images (CIFAR-10 32×32, FFHQ 256) with MSE/PSNR or MS-SSIM/LPIPS decoders, and none uses a semantic (DINOv2) branch. This leaves room for perceptual- and semantic-consistency claims at 256/512 ImageNet.

### Gaps
- We could not get CD3M or DCDDM full text, so their datasets, constellation orders and code availability are unknown.

## Q4. Are the channel inputs of the already-known works analog or digital?

### Takeaway
All of the known works send analog (continuous complex, power-normalised) channel inputs: SGD-JSCC, DiffJSCC, LRISC, Pei et al., DriftDecode, CDDM, DiffCom / HiFi-DiffCom, plus CDM-JSCC and Land-then-transport. QAM appears only in baselines, or, for Pei, in a figure caption that is never carried into the actual pipeline.

### Cited Findings
- **SGD-JSCC (arXiv:2501.01138, Jan 2025) [FT]: analog.** The transmitter "maps the source image x into a vector of complex-valued channel input symbols", and "An average transmit power constraint is imposed on z" (Sec. III-A). Finite constellations appear only as prior work (Sec. I-A, "transmission using a finite constellation"). Diffusion role: a denoiser of received features that "starts from the equalized channel output", "Unlike the generation-oriented reverse diffusion process which denoises from pure Gaussian noise" (Sec. V-B2). SNR is estimated blind and mapped to a starting step. COCO at 128×128 — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.01138)
- **DiffJSCC (arXiv:2404.17736, 2024) [FT]: analog.** "complex-valued channel input symbols"; "the final encoder layer normalizes the transmitted symbols" (Sec. II-A). 16-QAM and BPSK are used only in the BPG+LDPC baseline (Sec. III-C). Diffusion role: a generator, since "The diffusion process starts with a random input" and is conditioned on spatial, text and CSI features from the JSCC output (Sec. II-C). SNR-conditioned, assumes perfect CSI. OpenImages 256, Kodak, ADE20K, CelebA-HQ 512. Code: github.com/mingyuyng/DiffJSCC — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.17736)
- **LRISC (arXiv:2504.21577v2, Jun 2025) [FT]: analog.** "channel transmission symbol s∈ℂ^k" with an average power constraint. "Q denotes a 2^n-level scalar quantization" appears only in the bandwidth-cost model, and there is no QAM. A pixel-space conditional diffusion model is conditioned on the received latent. SNR-adaptive modules sit in the JSCC codec. COCO 256, Kodak. No code — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.21577)
- **Pei et al., LDM-enabled low-latency SemCom (arXiv:2406.06644v4, Feb 2025; accepted Jan 2025, journal not named on the page; the user lists it as TWC 2025) [FT]: effectively analog, despite the QAM wording.** The Fig. 1 caption says "represents the modulation encoding for 256-QAM", and Sec. II-C mentions "quadrature amplitude modulation (QAM)". But Algorithm 4 reads "Transmit real-valued z_R through noisy wireless channel", there is no quantiser, the word "constellation" never appears, and the experiments never mention QAM. Diffusion role: a latent denoiser whose start step comes from matching σ² to t_m² (Sec. IV-B). The decoder is a WGAN generator. Code: github.com/JianhuaPei/LDM-enabled-SemCom-system — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06644). Caveat: 46k of 146k characters were unread.
- **DriftDecode (Fu, Xiao, Skoglund; arXiv:2605.02325, 4 May 2026) [FT]: analog.** "maps the source image to a sequence of complex-valued channel symbols" satisfying "the average transmit-power constraint". The page contains no QAM, modulation, quantisation or constellation. It is not a diffusion receiver: a one-step SNR-FiLM U-Net with a drift-inspired loss, where diffusion and flow decoders are only latency baselines. DIV2K 256 crops and MNIST. No code — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02325)
- **CDDM (Wu, Chen, He, Qian, Xu, Tao, Zhang; arXiv:2309.08895, Sep 2023; GLOBECOM 2023 in part; the journal version is titled "...for Wireless Communications") [FT]: analog.** The real JSCC vector is grouped into complex symbols that "can be transmitted through the wireless channel". QAM appears only in the baseline ("quadrature amplitude modulations (QAM) adapted to the channel conditions" with DVB-T2 LDPC). Diffusion is a denoiser applied "after the channel equalization", starting "from the received signal rather than pure noise". Trained at 20 dB, with the step count adjusted per SNR. CIFAR-10 32 and DIV2K 256. No code stated — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.08895)
- **DiffCom / HiFi-DiffCom / Blind-DiffCom (Wang, Dai, Tan, Qin, Niu, Zhang; arXiv:2406.07390, Jun 2024, rev. Jan 2025; IEEE JSAC per search) [FT]: analog.** "encoding x to a vector of complex-valued channel-input symbols" (Sec. II). Digital modulation appears only in the baselines: "5G LDPC channel coding (code length 4096) and digital modulation in line with the 3GPP TS 38.214 standard" (Sec. IV-A4). DiffCom starts from pure noise with received-signal likelihood guidance. HiFi-DiffCom starts at an intermediate T_s "rather than starting from a pure Gaussian distribution". Blind-DiffCom estimates h. FFHQ and ImageNet at 256. Code: semcomm.github.io/DiffCom and github.com/wsxtyrdd/diffcom — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07390v2)
- **CDM-JSCC: Rate-Adaptive Generative SemCom Using Conditional Diffusion Models (Yang, Zhang, Cai; arXiv:2409.02597v3, Dec 2024) [FT]: analog.** s ∈ ℂ^k under a power constraint. Quantisation appears only in the rate allocation, and QAM only in BPG+LDPC. The diffusion decoder starts from "the randomly sampled Gaussian source", conditioned on the JSCC-recovered latent. One model per SNR. Code: github.com/zhang-guangyi/cdm-jscc — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.02597)
- **Land-then-transport (Fu, Xiao, Skoglund, Kim; arXiv:2601.07512, Jan 2026) [FT]: analog.** "the transmitter sends a clean image X₁∼q", and "the received signal is interpreted as a noisy sample at an effective landing time". LDPC is used only for the JPEG2000 baseline — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07512)
- Other analog flow/diffusion receivers seen in snippets only [SNIP], all apparently complex continuous symbols: channel-realization flow matching (arXiv:2607.24876), FlowSem (arXiv:2608.21651), restoration flow matching for MIMO (arXiv:2607.23615), ICDM (arXiv:2505.19983), ADDPS (arXiv:2604.16796), SC-CDM (arXiv:2410.02121; it uses a JPEG+LDPC+4-QAM baseline) — [2607.24876](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24876), [2608.21651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21651), [2410.02121](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.02121)

### Inferences
- The whole "generative diffusion JSCC receiver" literature, including our DiT-JSCC target, is analog. "Digital" appears in it only through separation baselines (LDPC + QAM). Pei et al. is the only one that uses QAM language for its own system, and its pipeline does not implement QAM. It should be described as nominally QAM, effectively analog.

### Gaps
- For Pei et al. and DiT-JSCC, the unread tail sections could in principle contain a digital experiment. This is unlikely, since the methods and experiment sections were read.

## Q5. Search log: what appears not to exist, and what remains uncertain

### Takeaway
After the searches below, the specific combination we want appears not to exist: a learned finite-constellation (M-QAM/PSK, DeepJSCC-Q-style) JSCC whose receiver is a pure-noise conditional diffusion transformer (DiT-JSCC-like) generating the image from the received constellation symbols. The adjacent combinations do exist: QAM-native generative JSCC without diffusion (SemQAM), diffusion symbol denoisers for VQ-QAM JSCC (SCDM, CD3M, DCDDM, SSCDM), and diffusion generators over classical-coded digital tokens (PDSC, TokCom, Qiao).

### Cited Findings

HiFi-DiffCom"; "\"digital\" generative semantic communication latent diffusion QAM bits LDPC image receiver conditioned"; "diffusion model JSCC finite constellation quantized symbols image transmission generative decoder 2025 2026"; "\"Channel denoising in digital semantic communication systems via discrete diffusion\" Tang"; "\"Residual vector quantized diffusion model\" ..."; "generative token communication masked discrete diffusion noisy channel image tokens QAM"; "flow matching receiver digital semantic communication QAM constellation image JSCC"; "\"DiT-JSCC\" Tan 2026 citing digital OR quantized OR QAM"; "CD3M ... None returned a DiT-JSCC-style digital system — see the papers cited in Q1–Q4, e.g. [SemQAM](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29643), [SSCDM](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.22983)
- Several search engines independently summarised the gap the same way. This is a search-tool synthesis, not a primary source, so it is supporting evidence only.
- We found no diffusion receiver that outputs or uses soft LLRs or soft demapper posteriors as its conditioning (search-level negative).

### Inferences
- Novelty claims that look defensible: (a) M-QAM soft-to-hard quantisation of a DiT-JSCC-style dual-branch (DINOv2 + detail) encoder, with a pure-noise conditional DiT receiver; (b) conditioning the DiT on demapped or soft constellation information rather than on analog symbols; (c) studying how constellation order and quantisation noise interact with the generative prior (perception/semantic consistency vs. M). Claims to avoid: "first digital diffusion SemCom" (SCDM, CD3M, SSCDM, PDSC exist) and "first QAM generative JSCC" (SemQAM, MOC-RVQ exist).

- 2510.26442: whether it uses 16-QAM digital transmission is unverified (snippet only).
- Citation graphs (Google Scholar or Semantic Scholar "cited by" for DiT-JSCC, DiffCom, DeepJSCC-Q) were not checked because of rate-limiting, so papers from Oct–Nov 2026 that cite DiT-JSCC could be missed. Re-run a Scholar "cited by" check on DiT-JSCC and SemQAM before submission.
