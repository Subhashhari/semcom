# Digital, token- and bit-based generative semantic communication for images (2022 to Nov 2026)

Scope: discrete-token, bit-level and separation-based generative SemCom for images, plus hybrids. Out of scope here (another researcher covers it): continuous JSCC symbols quantised to QAM with a diffusion receiver.

How the evidence was collected: full-text arXiv HTML pages were fetched with a tool that pulls out quotes and section names. Quotes below are what that tool returned. They are usually verbatim, but it is a summarising model, so check exact wording against the paper before quoting it in a publication. Entries marked **[abstract only, unverified]** were seen only through an abstract or search snippet. Dates come from arXiv IDs or version stamps.

---

## Objective: catalogue of verified works

### Takeaway
At least 12 works, verified from full text, send discrete tokens or bits over modelled digital links and reconstruct the image with a generative prior (MaskGIT, an autoregressive model, discrete diffusion, Stable Diffusion, or a GAN). None of them combines (a) foundation-model semantic features (DINOv2-like) quantised to tokens or bits, (b) a diffusion transformer that generates from pure noise conditioned on the decoded tokens, and (c) one model used across SNRs, especially without SNR knowledge.

### Cited Findings

**A. VQ or token indices over a digital link, with a generative token prior at the receiver**

- **TokCom: Token Communications** (Qiao et al., arXiv 2502.12096; v1 Feb 2025, v5 Jul 2026). VQGAN tokenizer from MaskGIT, "N=256 discrete tokens", "codebook size is Q=1024 and each token is represented by 10 bits", 256x256 ImageNet100. Channel mapping is classical separation: "tokens are grouped into packets, with each packet containing 4 tokens … rate-1/2 convolutional encoding with cyclic redundancy check … The 16-QAM modulation is used" (Sec. VI-A). Receiver: "For packets that experience errors, the corresponding token positions are marked with a special token, [MASK] … The decoder then iteratively predicts these masked tokens" (Sec. VI-B). SNRs tested were 6 to 9 dB, and the paper does not say whether one model is used across them. Metrics: CLIP, LPIPS, PSNR, TER. Code: github.com/liqiao19/TokenCom_Code. — [arXiv 2502.12096](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12096)
- **Text-Guided Token Communication** (Liu, Qiao et al., BIT; arXiv 2507.05781, 8 Jul 2025). TA-TiTok tokenizer, "codebook of size 8192, with each image represented by 128 tokens". Mapping: "8 tokens as a single package, resulting in 104 information bits per package (13 bits per token) and a code length of 256 bits", 5G NR polar coding with CRC, "4-QAM" (Sec. II-B, III-A). Receiver: a "pre-trained text-guided masked generative model (MaskGen)". Tokens are masked when "CRC_i = failure", then predicted "using an iterative confidence-based approach", with text guidance (Sec. II-D2). AWGN, −5 to 5 dB. Only the channel code is adjusted for new conditions: "only adjustment of channel coding parameters is needed" (Sec. III-D). Metrics: PSNR, LPIPS, CLIP. Baseline: ADJSCC. — [arXiv 2507.05781](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.05781)
- **ToDMA: Token-Domain Multiple Access** (Qiao, Mashhadi, Gao, Gündüz; arXiv 2502.06118, v2 10 Jul 2025). VQGAN with Q=1024 and N=256 at 256x256. Tokens are mapped **directly to codewords, with no bits**: "each token is modulated to a modulation codeword" (one-hot b, x=Ub; Sec. IV-A2). Positions where tokens collided become [MASK] and are predicted by a bidirectional transformer (MaskGIT ViT) restricted to the residual candidates (Sec. V-C/D). Rayleigh fading at a fixed 25 dB. Metrics: TER, PSNR, LPIPS. The follow-up is arXiv 2505.10946 **[abstract only, unverified]**. — [arXiv 2502.06118](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06118); [arXiv 2505.10946](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.10946)
- **STI-SemCom: Spatial Token Interleaving and Two-Pass BP Decoding** (Feng et al., Univ. Sydney; arXiv 2611.00697, 31 Oct 2026). Emu3 VQ tokenizer, "V = 16,384 and M = 14 bits per label", 512x512 images giving 4,096 tokens. The tokens are interleaved and mapped to 14-bit labels, then split into 7 blocks of 8,192 bits with 5G NR LDPC BG1 at R≈0.44, BPSK, AWGN. The Emu3-Gen autoregressive prior is fused with BP token scores (p^F ∝ p_BP·p_θ^λ) and turned into bit-level prior LLRs for a second BP pass. It fails at low SNR: "at −0.2 dB and below more than 40% of tokens stay wrong". Baselines are SwinJSCC and FAJSCC only, so there is no diffusion or analog generative comparison. Code: github.com/Yifan-Usyd/STI-SemCom. — [arXiv 2611.00697](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00697)
- **Semantic-Aware Generative Image Transmission for Visual IoT** (Zhang et al.; arXiv 2606.28398, 24 Jun 2026). Pre-trained VQGAN with a 24x24 grid and K=16384 at 384x384, "quantization precision of 14 bits", budgeted at 20 bits per kept token for channel-coding overhead. No specific code is named. "AWGN with QPSK modulation" plus Rayleigh fading. Only key tokens are sent. "MaskGIT uses Halton scheduling" fills the dropped tokens. The paper does not say how bit errors in received indices are handled. Metrics: PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, mAP. Code is on GitHub (zzzccy1/…). — [arXiv 2606.28398](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.28398)
- **TONIC** (Liu & Wang, Brunel; arXiv 2605.21553, 20 May 2026). VQ tokenizer with K=16384 and 576 tokens. "each token index is mapped to a fixed-length bit representation", using "fixed 16QAM modulation together with multiple LDPC code-rate options". A confidence gate turns unreliable tokens into erasures for a masked-completion prior, because "an incorrect token can be more harmful than an explicit erasure when a strong completion prior is available" (Sec. IV-C). The task is **classification, not reconstruction**. — [arXiv 2605.21553](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.21553v1)
- **SSCDM: Markov-Enforced Discrete Diffusion for Digital Semantic Symbol Error Correction** (Huh, Kang, Choi, SNU; arXiv 2603.22983, 24 Mar 2026). This is a JSCC encoder with a VQ codebook of size 16 matched to 16-QAM. The mapping is **direct index-to-constellation**: "Each codeword … corresponds to a symbol … in the constellation set" (Sec. III). A discrete diffusion model corrects index errors. The reverse process starts at a step chosen from the SNR: "k* = argmin |η − P/σ̄²|" (Sec. V-C). The model was trained at 20 dB and is applied at lower SNRs. FFHQ 256x256, MS-SSIM and LPIPS. Baselines: CD3M, DCDDM, SCDM, and VQ-JSCC. — [arXiv 2603.22983](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.22983v1)
- **Token Encoding for Semantic Recovery (TokCode)** (Hu, Wang, G. Y. Li; arXiv 2604.12931, Apr 2026). Uses an LLM-adapter token encoder at the transmitter against **token erasure**, evaluated on "token-based generative image transmission". There is no SNR model. **[abstract only, unverified]** — [arXiv 2604.12931](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12931)
- **H-TokCom: hierarchical token communication** (Lee, Oh, Park, Kim, Ko; arXiv 2604.27641, 30 Apr 2026). Uses a cluster-prefix plus token-suffix bit mapping with unequal power, evaluated on COCO at 3 dB. **[abstract only, unverified]** — [arXiv 2604.27641](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27641)

**B. VQ indices mapped straight to QAM, with a perceptual (GAN) decoder and no channel code**

- **MOC-RVQ** (Zhou, Sun, … Cui, Zhang; arXiv 2401.01272, Jan 2024, v2 Sep 2024). Uses an octonary multi-head codebook so that "N is set to 8 to directly align with the few states preference of 64-QAM". Indices go through "decimal-to-binary transformation and digital constellation modulation". There is no channel code: "we do not apply any channel error correction code for MOR-RVQ". The decoder is a VQGAN autoencoder with a Swin noise-reduction block and requantisation. AWGN from −5 to 30 dB, with the NRB fine-tuned on noisy channels. DIV2K at high resolution, PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS. Code: github.com/Albert2X/moc_rvq. — [arXiv 2401.01272](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.01272)
- **SemQAM: Joint Semantic-Channel Coding and Modulation for Generative Image Transmission** (Cao, Ying, Huang, Qin, Shen, Tsinghua; arXiv 2610.29643, 21 Oct 2026). RVQ with "1024 codewords whose indices are mapped one to one to Gray-labeled 1024-QAM symbols … without separate channel coding". The codebook is trained on detected indices. The decoder is a learned image codec (EF-LIC transforms) trained with "PatchGAN loss following VQGAN", so it is **not** diffusion. Metrics: LPIPS, DISTS, FID. Its baselines include "generative image codecs paired with LDPC and QAM" (MS-ILLM, OSCAR, OneDC, DiT-IC, EF-LIC, CoD-Lite), plus NTSCC, NTSCC+, MDJCM-A and Quad-JSCC. It does **not** compare with DiffJSCC, DiffCom or DiT-JSCC. Code is promised "upon acceptance". — [arXiv 2610.29643](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29643v1)

**C. Text, captions or Stable Diffusion latents sent as bits, with a Stable Diffusion receiver**

- **Latency-Aware Generative SemCom** (Qiao, Mashhadi, Gao, Foh, Xiao, Bennis; arXiv 2403.17256, Mar 2024). "The prompt is encoded to bits using UTF-8". The edge map is "compressed to bits using learned nonlinear transform coding". The prompt uses a rate-1/2 convolutional code with QPSK, and the edge map uses adaptive MQAM. A pre-trained Stable Diffusion model generates the image. The prompt is retransmitted on any CRC error. Metrics: CLIP and MS-SSIM. — [arXiv 2403.17256](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.17256)
- **Language-Oriented Semantic Latent Representation** (Cicchetti, Grassucci, Park, Choi, Barbarossa, Comminiello; arXiv 2405.09976, 16 May 2024). "each character is 8-bit ASCII coded and modulated using 16QAM". The latent embedding is "64-bit encoded as defined in the IEEE 754-2008". No channel code is described. Latent diffusion receiver, Flickr8k, LPIPS/FID/CLIP. Code: github.com/ispamm/Img2Img-SC. — [arXiv 2405.09976](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.09976v1)
- **Diffusion-Aided Bandwidth-Efficient SemCom with Adaptive Requests** (Wang, Xie, Li, Liu; arXiv 2510.26442, Oct 2025, v4 Jan 2026). Sends a caption (ASCII) plus sparse Stable Diffusion v2.1 VAE latent blocks (IEEE-754 floats). Both use rate-1/2 convolutional coding and 16-QAM over AWGN at 5 to 10 dB. Missing blocks are zero-filled, and caption-conditioned DDIM inpainting "clamps known sites and updates only masked sites". A ROUGE-L check triggers retransmission requests. The paper assumes "an accurate SNR estimate". — [arXiv 2510.26442](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.26442v4)
- **LLM-Based SemCom System for Image Transmission** (Ribouh, Saleem; arXiv 2501.12988, 22 Jan 2025). A LLaVA caption is sent with LDPC rate 0.5, 4-QAM and OFDM (Sionna). The receiver is a CLIP text encoder feeding Stable Diffusion. Corrupted captions are not explicitly corrected. — [arXiv 2501.12988](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.12988)

**D. Foundation-model or VLM tokens quantised to bits, with a diffusion receiver**

- **PDSC: Personalized Digital SemCom with VLMs** (Li et al., NUDT; arXiv 2608.14260, 14 Aug 2026). Tokens are LaVIT visual-semantic tokens, a BLIP caption, and a preference token, vector-quantised with K=1024 and L=21, coded as "fixed-length binary coding" for **210 bits per image**. The decoder is a latent diffusion model "built upon Stable Diffusion XL" with cross-attention, FiLM and text guidance. The channel code and modulation for PDSC are not specified ("conventional channel coding and modulation"). AWGN at 0 and 20 dB. Metrics: CLIP Image Score and CLIP Score. Baselines: BPG+LDPC, CDDM, MoS. — [arXiv 2608.14260](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14260)

**E. Digital separation with a generative decoder, compared directly with analog generative JSCC**

- **DiT-JSCC itself** (arXiv 2601.03112). Its separation baselines "combine them with 5G LDPC channel coding", where the learned codecs include "PerCo" and "DiffEIC". It uses "a 1/3 rate (4096, 6144) LDPC code with 4QAM" at 0 dB, plus 1/2 and 1/4 rates at other SNRs. It reports "the lowest-SNR points they can achieve … until the remaining CBR becomes insufficient for source coding". Result: "DiT-JSCC consistently outperforms all baselines". DiT-JSCC trains "separate models for each SNR". — [arXiv 2601.03112](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.03112)
- **DiffCom** (arXiv 2406.07390). Its baselines include "HiFiC [10], CDC [16], and MS-ILLM[17]" with "5G LDPC channel coding (code length 4096) and digital modulation in line with the 3GPP TS 38.214 standard". It finds that "Compared with CDC + 5G LDPC and MS-ILLM + 5G LDPC, our approach achieves better FID", and that "separation-based codecs (e.g., NIC + LDPC) suffer from noticeable cliff or leveling effects". The introduction concedes that end-to-end JSCC "still lag[s] behind in RDP performance by a substantial margin" relative to MS-ILLM + 5G LDPC. — [arXiv 2406.07390](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07390v2)
- **SemQAM** (see B) is the only verified work that **builds a digital generative scheme and benchmarks it at matched CBR/SNR** against six generative codecs + LDPC + QAM. It does not include analog generative JSCC. — [arXiv 2610.29643](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29643v1)

**F. Adjacent work: hybrids, erasures and bit-flip robustness**

- **Text-guided diffusion generative communication** (Chen et al., SJTU; arXiv 2510.21299, 24 Oct 2025). This is a **hybrid**: the image goes as analog JSCC symbols, while the prompt is "compressed using arithmetic coding, protected with LDPC channel coding" and sent "over an orthogonal channel". The receiver is Stable Diffusion 2.1 with ControlNet. Baselines: DiffJSCC, SwinJSCC, MambaJSCC. — [arXiv 2510.21299](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21299)
- **ResiTok** (Liu, Ma, Tafazolli; arXiv 2505.01870, 3 May 2025). TiTok tokens with a 4096 codebook (12 bits), Turbo coding and AMC. Untransmitted detail tokens are zero-padded. The decoder is a ViT (**not generative**). — [arXiv 2505.01870](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.01870)
- **LGSemCom** (Bo, Zhang, Tao et al.; arXiv 2609.06989, 7 Sep 2026). Uses a learnable bit mapper over packet erasures. Lost bits become a learnable token, and a GAN decoder treats recovery "as a conditional generative task". SNR is not addressed. — [arXiv 2609.06989](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06989)
- **ResiComp** (Wang, Dai et al.; arXiv 2502.10812, IEEE TCSVT 2025). A masked visual token transformer serves both as entropy model and as packet-loss concealment in a neural codec. **[abstract/snippet only, unverified]** — [arXiv 2502.10812](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.10812v2)
- **ResiGLC** (Yao, Dai et al.; arXiv 2608.19590, Aug 2026, accepted to IEEE TMC). A loss-resilient generative latent coding scheme with masked learning and a multimodal prior, evaluated under packet loss. **[abstract only, unverified]** — [arXiv 2608.19590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19590)
- **Bit-flip robustness of diffusion codecs** (Vaisman, Pomerants, Lapid; arXiv 2604.05743, AIGENS@CVPR 2026). Reports that reverse-channel-coding diffusion codecs (Turbo-DDCM) are "considerably more tolerant of bit flips". **[abstract only, unverified]** — [arXiv 2604.05743](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05743)
- **BitFlat** (arXiv 2610.25682). An error-localised bitstream for diffusion image compression using parity, Hamming and SECDED codes. **[snippet only, unverified]** — [arXiv 2610.25682](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.25682)
- **Generative Feature Imputing** (Huang, Zeng, Du, K. Huang; arXiv 2508.17957, Aug 2025). A diffusion model "reconstruct[s] missing features caused by packet losses" in digital SemCom. **[abstract only, unverified]** — [arXiv 2508.17957](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17957)
- **STCC** (Bao, Dong et al.; arXiv 2606.11819, 10 Jun 2026). Maps MaskGIT/VQGAN tokens to a **learned, semantically structured constellation** that is analog-like, with no bits. The receiver is a transformer plus nearest-codeword lookup, so it is **not generative**. Training samples SNR uniformly from 5 to 15 dB. — [arXiv 2606.11819](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11819)
- **VQ-DeepISC** (Chen et al.; arXiv 2508.03740, Aug 2025). Indices are "mapped into a bit sequence", with QPSK and OFDM (802.11a). An SNR ModNet lets one model cover 0 to 15 dB. The decoder is trained on MSE, so it is **not generative**. — [arXiv 2508.03740](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.03740v1)
- **SQ-GAN** (Pezone, Barbarossa, Caire; arXiv 2502.09520, Feb 2025). Masked VQ (1024 codewords) with a GAN decoder conditioned on a segmentation map, on Cityscapes. "No noisy channel is modelled"; rate is given in bpp only. Code: github.com/frapez1/SQ-GAN. — [arXiv 2502.09520](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.09520v1)
- **M2GSC** (Yang et al.; arXiv 2408.08765, Aug 2024). The abstract claims compatibility with digital transmission, but the full text has no quantiser, channel code or modulation; the case study uses a Gaussian channel. — [arXiv 2408.08765](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.08765v1)
- **Large AI Model-Enabled Generative SemCom** (Ma, Ni, Qin; arXiv 2509.21394, Sep 2025). The transmitted signal is real-valued over AGN. LDPC appears only in the BPG+LDPC baseline, so this is **not digital**. — [arXiv 2509.21394](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.21394)

### Inferences
- The dominant digital-generative pattern is **classical separation at the bit layer**: token index → fixed-length bits → LDPC, polar or convolutional code with CRC → QAM. CRC failures become [MASK] or erasures, and a pre-trained masked or autoregressive token prior fills them in (TokCom, TG-TokCom, TONIC, IoT-MaskGIT, STI-SemCom). The second pattern is **direct index-to-QAM mapping with no FEC** (MOC-RVQ, SemQAM, SSCDM, ToDMA's codeword mapping).
- No verified work uses a **continuous-latent diffusion model or a DiT that generates from noise** conditioned on decoded discrete tokens over a modelled noisy digital link, apart from Stable Diffusion receivers conditioned on captions, latents or VLM tokens (PDSC, adaptive requests, latency-aware). All token-domain works use MaskGIT, MaskGen or Emu3 priors, or VQGAN/GAN decoders.

### Gaps
- Not fetched in full: ESC-MVQ (arXiv 2504.11709), euJSCC (arXiv 2602.14018), Universal JSCC (arXiv 2405.10749), VQ-DeepSC (arXiv 2209.11519), Masked VQ-VAE (arXiv 2206.04011, IEEE 2023, a classification task), SPIC (arXiv 2310.15737, ICASSP 2024), the TechRxiv "Generative AI-based VQ E2E SemCom" (HTTP 403), MoST (arXiv 2509.09499), Gen-SC (arXiv 2409.17104, WCSP 2024), Token-Oriented SemCom with pretrained ViTs (arXiv 2608.25410), and TokenCom/TaiChi (arXiv 2603.00482). TaiChi's abstract mentions "joint VLM-channel coding" but says nothing about a generative receiver. The original CD3M and DCDDM papers were not located; they are known only as SSCDM's baselines.
- MOC-RVQ, TokCom and SemQAM venues: not confirmed (only arXiv versions were seen).

## Q1: Which works transmit VQ token indices over noisy digital channels and use a generative model to correct or complete corrupted tokens? How do they map tokens to modulation?

### Takeaway
This is an established line of work, mostly from 2025–2026 and mostly led by Li Qiao and Zhen Gao's group (TokCom, TG-TokCom, ToDMA). Tokens usually become fixed-length bits under a classical FEC, and CRC failures are masked and filled by MaskGIT or MaskGen. A discrete diffusion corrector (SSCDM) and an autoregressive-prior-aided LDPC decoder (STI-SemCom) also exist. "Generative token completion over a digital channel" is therefore not novel by itself.

### Cited Findings
- TokCom: "rate-1/2 convolutional encoding with cyclic redundancy check … 16-QAM". Erroneous packets are "marked with a special token, [MASK]" and predicted by MaskGIT. — [arXiv 2502.12096](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12096)
- TG-TokCom: 13 bits per token, 8 tokens per 256-bit polar codeword, 4-QAM. CRC failure leads to [MASK] and text-guided MaskGen. — [arXiv 2507.05781](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.05781)
- ToDMA: "each token is modulated to a modulation codeword" (direct token-to-codeword). Collided positions are filled by a MaskGIT-style bidirectional transformer. — [arXiv 2502.06118](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06118)
- STI-SemCom: 14-bit labels, 5G NR LDPC, BPSK. Emu3-Gen autoregressive token probabilities are fused with BP scores and fed back as bit prior LLRs for a second BP pass. — [arXiv 2611.00697](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00697)
- SSCDM: "Each codeword … corresponds to a symbol … in the constellation set" (16 codewords for 16-QAM). A Markov-consistent discrete diffusion model corrects symbol errors. It cites prior discrete-diffusion correctors CD3M ("applies D3PM … for symbol-level error correction") and DCDDM. — [arXiv 2603.22983](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.22983v1)
- MOC-RVQ: 8-state codebooks "to directly align with … 64-QAM", "we do not apply any channel error correction code". It uses a VQGAN decoder with a learned noise-reduction block and requantisation. — [arXiv 2401.01272](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.01272)
- SemQAM: "indices are mapped one to one to Gray-labeled 1024-QAM symbols … without separate channel coding". — [arXiv 2610.29643](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29643v1)
- IoT MaskGIT: 14-bit indices budgeted at 20 bits per token with QPSK. MaskGIT with Halton scheduling fills tokens that were deliberately not sent. Bit errors in the kept tokens are not handled. — [arXiv 2606.28398](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.28398)
- TONIC: bit mapping with 16QAM and LDPC rate options. Confidence gating converts low-confidence tokens to erasures, because a wrong token is worse than an erasure under a strong prior. The task is classification. — [arXiv 2605.21553](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.21553v1)

### Inferences
- The verified receivers handle corruption in four ways: (i) CRC → mask → masked-token prediction, (ii) confidence → erasure → completion, (iii) iterative discrete diffusion on indices, and (iv) prior-aided soft channel decoding. Option (iv) is the only one that feeds the generative prior back into the channel decoder.
- Every verified receiver works in the **discrete token domain** (MaskGIT, Emu3, D3PM-style). None uses decoded tokens or bits only as a *condition* for a continuous diffusion or DiT generator that starts from noise, which is what a digital DiT-JSCC would do.

### Gaps
- No verified work runs a discrete diffusion model (D3PM, MDLM) over **large-codebook image tokens** (1024 to 16k) for channel-error correction. SSCDM's codebook has 16 entries, and the CD3M and DCDDM originals were not located.
- No verified work combines VQ tokens with a DINOv2-style semantic branch.

## Q2: Which works use generative compression followed by a classical channel code (digital separation with a generative decoder)? Do any compare directly with analog generative JSCC at matched bandwidth?

### Takeaway
As baselines, digital separation with a generative codec is standard: PerCo, DiffEIC, HiFiC, CDC, MS-ILLM, OSCAR, OneDC and DiT-IC, each with 5G LDPC and QAM. The two key analog generative JSCC papers (DiT-JSCC and DiffCom) already compare against such pipelines, at their own operating points and reporting the "lowest SNR achievable". As **proposed systems**, separation-style generative schemes are mainly text- or caption-based (latency-aware, Img2Img-SC, adaptive requests, LLM-based) and token-based (TokCom family, STI-SemCom). None of these proposed digital generative systems has been benchmarked against DiT-JSCC, DiffCom or DiffJSCC at matched CBR and SNR.

### Cited Findings
- DiT-JSCC: PerCo and DiffEIC (plus BPG and VTM) with "5G LDPC channel coding", "1/3 rate (4096, 6144) LDPC code with 4QAM" at 0 dB. The baselines are pushed down "until the remaining CBR becomes insufficient for source coding". At 0 dB Rayleigh, "The available bandwidth is insufficient to support BPG encoding". — [arXiv 2601.03112](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.03112)
- DiffCom: HiFiC, CDC and MS-ILLM paired with "5G LDPC channel coding (code length 4096) and digital modulation in line with the 3GPP TS 38.214 standard". It has better FID than CDC+LDPC and MS-ILLM+LDPC, but "these results are expected since HiFiC, CDC, and MS-ILLM utilize LPIPS as a loss function". Separation shows "noticeable cliff or leveling effects". — [arXiv 2406.07390](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07390v2)
- SemQAM: generative codecs "paired with LDPC and QAM" (MS-ILLM, OSCAR, OneDC, DiT-IC, EF-LIC, CoD-Lite). SemQAM-C is "the separated system combining the noiseless source codec with LDPC and QAM". It does not compare with analog diffusion JSCC. — [arXiv 2610.29643](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29643v1)
- Diffusion-codec bit-flip robustness: diffusion codecs based on reverse channel coding are "considerably more tolerant of bit flips" and "might reduce the need for error-correcting codes". **[abstract only, unverified]** — [arXiv 2604.05743](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05743)
- Text and latent bit pipelines with a Stable Diffusion receiver: ASCII/UTF-8 text, IEEE-754 latents, convolutional or LDPC codes, and QAM. — [arXiv 2403.17256](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.17256); [arXiv 2405.09976](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.09976v1); [arXiv 2510.26442](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.26442v4); [arXiv 2501.12988](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.12988)
- Hybrid (analog image plus digital text): the prompt is "compressed using arithmetic coding, protected with LDPC" and sent on an orthogonal channel. It is compared with DiffJSCC, but not with a digital generative separation scheme. — [arXiv 2510.21299](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21299)

### Inferences
- A "digital DiT-JSCC" would sit in a cell that is crowded with baselines but has few proposed systems. Claiming "digital generative separation beats or matches analog generative JSCC" would contradict DiT-JSCC's own Fig. 10/11 and Table II results, so it would need a new, fair, matched-CBR study. Including **DiT-JSCC as a baseline against a digital token or bit scheme at matched CBR** appears to be unreported.
- DiffCom's concession that MS-ILLM + 5G LDPC beats end-to-end JSCC in rate-distortion-perception (at high SNR, before the cliff) suggests that a strong digital generative baseline is a fair and expected comparison.

### Gaps
- No proposed digital generative system with published results was found that compares against DiffJSCC, DiffCom or DiT-JSCC at matched CBR and SNR.
- DiT-JSCC's own CBR-matching method for its baselines is only partly described: it reports the lowest achievable SNR rather than matched CBR.

## Q3: Which works quantise foundation-model semantic features (DINOv2, CLIP, BLIP captions) to bits or tokens, send them digitally, and generate the image with diffusion at the receiver?

### Takeaway
Captions (BLIP, LLaVA, GPT-4, Molmo) sent as bits to a Stable Diffusion receiver are well established. PDSC (Aug 2026) vector-quantises LaVIT visual tokens plus a BLIP caption to 210 bits for an SDXL decoder. No work quantises **DINOv2** features to tokens or bits for a digital link with a diffusion receiver, and none quantises CLIP image embeddings to bits for an unCLIP-style receiver.

### Cited Findings
- PDSC: tokens are LaVIT visual-semantic tokens, a BLIP caption and a preference token, VQ with K=1024 and L=21, "fixed-length binary coding", giving 210 bits per image. Decoder "built upon Stable Diffusion XL". The channel code is unspecified, and there is no mechanism for handling corrupted indices. ### Inferences
- A digital DiT-JSCC that sends a **quantised DINOv2 semantic token stream plus a quantised detail stream** (bits with FEC, or index-to-QAM) to a DiT that generates from noise appears unreported. Its novelty claim should be scoped to that specific combination, not to "digital generative SemCom" or "foundation features as bits".

## Q4: Does any work address SNR adaptivity (one model across SNRs, with or without SNR knowledge) in a digital generative image scheme?

### Takeaway
SNR adaptivity is addressed only partially, and always with the SNR known. SemQAM uses one shared model with per-SNR codebooks. SSCDM uses one discrete diffusion model whose start step is set from the SNR. TONIC needs no per-SNR retraining but calibrates thresholds for each deployment, and its task is classification. ResiTok and TG-TokCom adapt only the MCS or channel code. No verified digital generative image scheme is **SNR-blind**, and none conditions a diffusion generator on SNR.

### Cited Findings
- SemQAM: "SemQAM uses four CBR settings for each of 10 dB, 16 dB, 24 dB, and noiseless transmission, giving sixteen codebook groups". It "shares the image transforms, hyperprior, and context networks while selecting the corresponding VQ codebooks". — [arXiv 2610.29643](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29643v1)
- SSCDM: "the training SNR is fixed at η_train = 20 dB … while the DM is applied under lower SNR regimes". The start step is "k* = argmin |η − P/σ̄²|", so the SNR must be known. — [arXiv 2603.22983](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.22983v1)
- TONIC: "no per-SNR or per-channel end-to-end retraining is performed", but "this calibration is carried out for the target deployment condition". — [arXiv 2605.21553](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.21553v1)
- TG-TokCom: "only adjustment of channel coding parameters is needed". — [arXiv 2507.05781](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.05781)
- ResiTok: "we choose the best-performing configuration of modulation and coding rate based on the AMC in the standard". — [arXiv 2505.01870](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.01870)
- STI-SemCom: channel LLRs are "−2y/σ²", which implies the noise variance is known. The paper does not state whether one model covers all SNRs. — [arXiv 2611.00697](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.00697)
- Adaptive requests: the paper "assumes the receiver has an accurate SNR estimate", and the inpainting strength heuristic decreases as SNR rises. — [arXiv 2510.26442](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.26442v4)
- For contrast, from non-generative digital work: VQ-DeepISC's ModNet "dynamically generates scaling parameters conditioned on the instantaneous SNR", with one model trained over 0 to 15 dB. — [arXiv 2508.03740](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.03740v1)
- For contrast, from analog generative JSCC: DiT-JSCC trains "separate models for each SNR", and DiffCom takes the noise power σn² as input to pick its start timestep. — [arXiv 2601.03112](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.03112); [arXiv 2406.07390](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07390v2)

### Inferences
- In CRC-masking token schemes (TokCom, TG-TokCom), the generative stage is SNR-agnostic by construction, since it sees only erasures. SNR enters only through rate and MCS choice and the residual erasure rate. "One generative model across SNRs" is therefore near-trivial in that design. A non-trivial claim would be a single model that degrades gracefully at low SNR, where CRC or BP fails massively (STI-SemCom breaks down below −0.2 dB), especially without SNR knowledge.
- A digital DiT-JSCC trained once over a range of SNRs or erasure and error rates, possibly blind (inferring reliability from soft LLRs or confidences rather than from a given SNR), would not overlap with any verified work. TONIC's confidence gating is the closest idea, but it is for classification.

### Gaps
- No verified paper reports a blind-SNR digital generative image scheme.
- SNR-sweep training details for TokCom, the IoT MaskGIT paper and PDSC are not stated in their text.
